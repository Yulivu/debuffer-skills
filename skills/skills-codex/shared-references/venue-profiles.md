# Venue Profiles

Use this file when planning, reviewing, or writing for a target venue. Keep the
profile concise and combine it with the venue's current official instructions.

## ICLR

- Optimize for a fast open-review read: strong abstract, early motivation,
  clear contribution list, and a figure that explains the method quickly.
- Reviewers scrutinize novelty, empirical rigor, ablations, reproducibility,
  and limitations. Include negative results when they clarify the claim.
- Make the review prompt ask for borderline-rejection reasons and missing
  experiments that would change a score.

## AAAI

- Write for a broad AI audience, not only a narrow subcommunity.
- Prioritize clarity of problem setting, technical soundness, significance,
  reproducibility, and responsible-AI/ethics checklist readiness.
- Avoid over-specialized framing; explain why the contribution matters beyond
  the immediate benchmark or architecture.

## JMLR

- Treat it as a journal article: completeness, depth, careful related work,
  full derivations, robust experiments, and mature exposition matter more than
  deadline-style compression.
- Review should test whether the paper is self-contained, whether claims are
  exhaustive enough for a journal, and whether all important baselines and
  assumptions are addressed.
- Prefer a long-form plan with appendices and reproducibility details, but keep
  working notes compact until the manuscript phase.

## TPAMI

- Treat it as a mature vision/ML journal venue with high expectations for
  novelty, technical depth, benchmark coverage, and polished writing.
- Related work should be broad and historically aware; experiments should show
  robustness across datasets, settings, and failure cases.
- For extensions of conference work, make the incremental contribution explicit
  and substantial: new theory, new method, broader experiments, or deeper
  analysis.

## NeurIPS / ICML

- Emphasize generality, rigorous empirical design, ablations, calibration of
  claims, and reproducibility.
- Ask reviewers to separate "interesting idea" from "sufficient evidence" and
  to identify the minimum additional experiment for acceptance lift.

## IEEE Journal / Conference

- Follow the exact template, reference style, and page constraints.
- Prioritize engineering reproducibility, clear system diagrams, quantitative
  comparisons, and conservative claims.
